### Goal

Monitor the deployed application using Amazon CloudWatch.

---

## 1. Overview

In this chapter, you will use Amazon CloudWatch to monitor the health and performance of the deployed application.

Amazon CloudWatch collects container logs, ECS resource metrics, and Application Load Balancer metrics, allowing you to observe application behavior, troubleshoot issues, and verify that the service is running correctly.
